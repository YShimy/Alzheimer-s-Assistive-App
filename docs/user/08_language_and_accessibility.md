# 08 — Language and Accessibility

The Cognitive and Memory Support App is built with accessibility as its core philosophy. We believe that technology should adapt to the patient, not the other way around.

---

## 1. Full Arabic and English Support

This application is fully bilingual, supporting both **Arabic** and **English**. 

### Why This Matters
For people with cognitive challenges, interacting with technology in their native, most comfortable language is essential. Forcing a native Arabic speaker to navigate an English app adds unnecessary mental strain. 

### What "Full Support" Means
This is not just translated text. When you switch the app to Arabic:
*   **RTL Layout:** The entire screen flips to a Right-to-Left (RTL) layout. Navigation arrows, buttons, and menus move to the correct side for an Arabic reader.
*   **Eastern Arabic Numerals:** Where appropriate (like in progress streaks), the app will use Eastern Arabic numbers (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩) instead of Western numbers.
*   **Respectful Phrasing:** The translations are chosen to be calm, culturally appropriate, and supportive.

### Independent Language Choice
The patient and the caregiver can choose their languages independently. A patient can use the app comfortably in Arabic on their iPad, while their caregiver manages the schedule in English on their own phone.

You can change the language at any time from the app's settings. *(Note: On some Android devices, changing from English to Arabic may require you to briefly close and restart the app so the layout can flip correctly).*

---

## 2. Design Accessibility Principles

Beyond language, the app uses specific design choices to reduce cognitive load:

*   **Big, Obvious Actions:** Buttons for "Done" or "Start Now" are large and clearly separated.
*   **No Clutter:** The patient's "Home" view only shows what is happening *today*. Tomorrow's tasks are hidden so they don't cause anxiety.
*   **High Contrast and Readability:** Colors are chosen to be clear, and text is sized to be readable without squinting.
*   **No Dead Ends:** If a patient taps into a checklist or a game, there is always an obvious, safe way to go "Back" or exit.

Our goal is that a patient should never feel "trapped" or confused by a screen. The app should feel like a quiet, helpful assistant.

# 09 — Common Tasks & FAQ

Here are simple answers to the most common questions about using the app.

---

## For Caregivers

### How do I link to a patient?
1. Open your app and go to the **Patients** tab.
2. Tap "Generate Invite Code".
3. Give that 6-letter code to the patient.
4. Have the patient enter it on their profile screen.

### How do I create a Reminder?
1. Make sure you have selected a patient on your dashboard.
2. Go to the **Manage Reminders** screen.
3. Tap the "Add" or "+" button.
4. Fill in the title, time, and how often it repeats. 
5. Tap Save. The reminder will sync to the patient's phone.

### What is the difference between Normal, High, and Critical priority?
"Normal" is for everyday tasks. "High" and "Critical" are for essential tasks (like medication). If a patient skips a High or Critical task, it will trigger an "Escalation Alert" in your Progress tab so you can follow up with them.

### Why didn't my new reminder show up on the patient's phone immediately?
If you create a reminder, the patient's phone needs an internet connection to download it. It will usually update the next time they open the app. 

---

## For Patients

### What does "Snooze" mean?
If a reminder pops up and you are busy (for example, you are in the bathroom or talking to a friend), tap "Snooze." The app will go quiet and gently remind you again in a few minutes.

### How do I start a Checklist?
You can start a checklist in two ways:
1. Tap the **Checklists** button at the bottom of your screen, pick a routine, and tap Start.
2. Sometimes, a reminder will have a "Start Now" button. Tapping that will launch the checklist for you automatically.

### Can I change the language?
Yes! You can switch the entire app to Arabic or English. Go to your settings/profile area and look for the language option.

## Troubleshooting

**The app layout looks weird after I changed it to Arabic.**
If the buttons didn't flip to the right side, just close the app completely (swipe it away on your phone) and open it again. The Right-to-Left layout will fix itself on the fresh start.

**A photo I uploaded isn't showing up on the patient's phone.**
In the current research version of the app, media (like photos and videos) requires a specific network setup. Ensure both phones have internet access. If you are testing the app locally, see the "Known Limits" guide.

**I missed a reminder, what happens?**
Nothing bad happens! The app simply records that it was missed. You can just catch the next one. Don't worry about being perfect.

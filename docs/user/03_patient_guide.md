# 03 — Patient Guide

Welcome to your daily companion app. 

This app is here to help you keep track of your day, remember important things, and stay connected with the people who care about you. It is designed to be calm, easy to read, and simple to use.

---

## What You Will See

When you open the app, you will see a simple **Home View**. This screen tells you exactly what is happening today. You don't need to dig through menus to find what you need.

The app is divided into a few simple areas at the bottom of your screen:

1.  **Home:** Your quick summary of today.
2.  **Reminders:** Things to do.
3.  **Checklists:** Step-by-step guides.
4.  **Games:** Fun, light activities for your brain.

---

## Handling Reminders

Throughout the day, your phone will gently notify you when it is time to do something, like take a medication or drink some water. 

When a reminder pops up, you have three simple choices:

*   **Done:** Tap this when you have finished the task. Great job!
*   **Snooze:** Tap this if you are busy right now. The app will remind you again in a little while.
*   **Skip:** Tap this if you cannot do the task today. That is perfectly fine. The app will just catch you next time.

Sometimes, a reminder might have a button that says **"Start Now."** This means the reminder is attached to a Checklist, which will guide you through the task step-by-step.

---

## Using Checklists

Some tasks have a few steps, like getting ready in the morning. Checklists make these easy.

When you open a checklist, the app will show you one step at a time.
1. Read the step.
2. If there is a photo, look at the photo to help you.
3. Do the task.
4. Tap the button to move to the next step.

Take all the time you need. There is no rush.

---

## Your Contacts

The app holds a special list of your important people. You might see a photo of them, their name, and a helpful little note (like "Your grandson"). You can use this to feel close to your family and quickly remember who is who.

---

## Tracking Your Progress

If you visit the **Progress** screen, you will see your "Streaks." A streak is just how many days in a row you have used the app. 

This screen is here to encourage you. It is not a test, and there are no bad grades. Every day you use the app is a good day. If you miss a day, don't worry—you can just start a new streak tomorrow!

---

## 🌍 Using the App in Arabic or English

You can use this entire app in either **Arabic** or **English**. 

If you choose Arabic, the app will completely adjust for you. The text will read from Right-to-Left, the buttons will move to the comfortable side, and the numbers will look familiar. You can change the language at any time in your settings. Choose whichever language feels most like home to you.

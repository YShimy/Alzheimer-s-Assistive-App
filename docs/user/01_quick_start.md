# 01 — Quick Start Guide

Welcome to the **Cognitive and Memory Support Application**.

This app is designed to provide gentle, structured daily support for people living with early-stage Alzheimer’s disease. It helps manage daily routines, remember important tasks, and stay connected with loved ones.

---

## What the App Is For
The app is a **daily companion**. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Instead, it offers practical help with:
*   **Knowing what to do today:** Seeing upcoming tasks clearly.
*   **Following routines:** Getting step-by-step guidance for complex tasks like making coffee or getting ready for bed.
*   **Staying engaged:** Playing light, supportive memory games.
*   **Connecting with support:** Allowing a trusted caregiver to help manage the schedule from afar.

## Who Uses the App?
The app is built for two specific roles. The experience looks very different depending on which role you choose during setup.

### 1. The Patient (Primary User)
This is the person who uses the app every day. The patient’s screen is calm, simple, and focused only on the present moment. They receive gentle reminders, follow checklists, play games, and view their personal contacts. The design removes clutter to keep cognitive load as low as possible.

### 2. The Caregiver (Support User)
This is a family member or professional carer. The caregiver uses the app to set up the patient's digital environment. From their own device, a caregiver can add reminders, create checklists, and check in on how consistently the patient is completing their daily tasks.

*Note: The app requires the caregiver to create an "Invite Code" and share it with the patient to link their accounts together securely.*

---

## The Main Features
*   **Reminders:** Notifications for medications, appointments, or simple tasks. They can be marked as "Done," "Snoozed," or "Skipped."
*   **Checklists:** Step-by-step guides for routines. Caregivers can attach photos to help explain a step.
*   **Contacts:** A safe, organized list of important people, complete with photos and short memos (like "Your eldest daughter") added by the caregiver.
*   **Games:** Two simple, engaging games (Safe Path Memory and Routine Sequence) designed to offer light mental stimulation without pressure.
*   **Progress:** A supportive summary showing consistency and "streaks" (consecutive days of activity) to encourage daily use.

---

## 🌟 Full Arabic and English Support
One of the most important strengths of this app is that it is built from the ground up to support both **Arabic** and **English**. 

When you switch the app to Arabic, the entire layout flips to Right-to-Left (RTL), making it feel natural and comfortable. Numbers are displayed using Eastern Arabic numerals, and the wording is chosen to be respectful and clear. 

Language can be switched at any time, allowing a patient to use the app in Arabic while their caregiver might prefer English on their own device.

---

## How to Get Started
1. **Download and Open the App:** Start the app and create an account using your email.
2. **Choose Your Role:** Tell the app if you are the Patient or the Caregiver.
3. **Link Together:** The caregiver generates an Invite Code on their screen. The patient enters this code on their profile screen.
4. **Start Small:** The caregiver should add just one or two simple reminders (like "Drink water") to help the patient get used to the app without feeling overwhelmed.

*Note: The current version of the app is a research prototype. You may notice some features require specific network setups if you are testing it, and actions require an internet connection to sync. See the "Known Limits" guide for details.*

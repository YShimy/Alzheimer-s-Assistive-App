# 03 — Patient Guide

Welcome to your daily support app.

The app is designed to help you keep track of your day, remember important tasks, and follow routines in a calm and simple way.

---

## What You Will See

The patient side of the app is intended to stay simple.

In the current version, the patient will mainly use screens for:

1. **Home / Today** — a simple view of what is happening today
2. **Reminders** — things that need attention
3. **Checklists** — step-by-step task support
4. **Games** — light activities for engagement
5. **Progress** — encouraging summaries and streak-style feedback where supported

---

## Reminders

When a reminder appears, the app may offer actions such as:

- **Done** — you finished the task
- **Snooze** — remind me again later
- **Skip** — I am not doing this now

In some versions or flows, a reminder may also connect to a checklist so that the task can be started step by step.

---

## Checklists

Checklists are for tasks with more than one step.

They are meant to make routines easier by showing one step at a time. Where supported, a step may also include a helpful photo or media cue.

---

## Contacts and Memory Support

If the current version includes contacts or memory-aid features, you may see important people with names, photos, and short helpful notes.

These are meant to help you feel more confident and connected.

---

## Progress

The progress area is meant to be encouraging.

It may show things like:

- whether tasks were completed regularly
- streaks or activity patterns
- simple game or routine summaries

It is not a test or a grade. It is there to support regular use.

---

## Arabic and English

The app supports both **Arabic** and **English**. You can use the language that feels most comfortable.

In Arabic mode, the app is designed to follow right-to-left reading and layout patterns where supported in the current implementation.

---

## Current-Version Note

This app is still being developed as a research prototype. Some screens or features may still be improving, but the main goal stays the same: make daily support simpler and calmer.
